# ⛏️ Webcraft2D

**Webcraft2D** is an action-packed, feature-rich 2D browser-based sandbox survival game inspired by Minecraft. Built from scratch using modern vanilla Web technologies, Webcraft2D combines classic block-building creativity with survival mechanics, procedural world exploration, crafting pipelines, animal husbandry, and native controller support—all running smoothly directly in your web browser.

---

## 🌟 Game Features Overview

### 🌍 World Generation & Exploration
* **Procedural Biomes**: Explore dynamically generated worlds featuring grassy plains, dense oak forests, sandy deserts, snowy tundras, and lush tropical **Jungle Biomes** with towering trees, climbable vines, and wild melon patches.
* **Day & Night Cycle & Sky Raycasting**: Experience seamless day-night cycles with natural sky raycasting, preventing false shadow pillars and letting exterior sunlight flood into cave mouths.
* **Terraria-Style Subterranean Caverns**: Plunge into deep, interconnected underground cave networks enveloped by atmospheric backdrop walls and inky black depth shadows starting below surface coal seams.
* **Mineral Wealth**: Mine underground to discover rich seams of Coal, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Redstone, and Lapis Lazuli embedded deep in stone strata.

### ⛏️ Mining, Tools & Progression
* **Authentic Mining Tiers**: Gear up across 5 material tiers: **Wood, Stone, Iron, Gold, and Diamond**. Lower tier tools cannot harvest high-grade ores, enforcing authentic progression.
* **Full Toolset**: Craft Pickaxes, Shovels, Axes, Swords, and Hoes—each optimized with specific mining speeds, durability, and block drop rules.
* **10-Stage Block Fracture**: Authentic pixelated crack animations visually indicate block damage as you mine.

### 🌾 Agriculture, Farming & Cooking
* **Tillable Farmland**: Use hoes to convert soil into fertile farmland near water sources to accelerate crop growth.
* **Progressive Crop Cultivation**: Plant Wheat Seeds and cultivate wild Melons across authentic multi-stage growth cycles.
* **Baking & Cooking**: Cook raw meats in a furnace, slice fresh melons, or bake harvested wheat into golden Bread with revamped high-definition pixel-art food textures.

### 🐄 Livestock, Avian Wildlife & Companions
* **Animal Husbandry**: Encounter peaceful Cows, Pigs, Sheep, and Chickens roaming the surface biomes with realistic procedural leg cycles and head bobs.
* **Exotic Jungle Parrots**: Discover wild Parrots in 5 vibrant color morphs (Red, Blue, Green, Cyan, Gray). Tame them with seeds to make them perch loyally upon your shoulder!
* **Sweet Avian Pigeons**: Watch wild Pigeons soar above canopies with authentic flight aerodynamics, perch on leaves, forage for seeds, and respond to flocking calls.
* **Resource Production**: Harvest livestock for Raw Meats, Leather, and Wool, or milk cows with buckets. Birds are aesthetic companions—hurting them yields no drops and unlocks bittersweet achievements.

### 🌀 Dimensional Exploration, Kael & The Atlas Outpost
* **Kael, The Atlas Explorer**: Experience the cinematic arrival of dimensional cartographer Kael through a swirling pixel rift portal, settling permanently into your world.
* **Planar Sanctuary Ward**: Kael radiates a 4.5-block defensive ward that pushes hostile mobs away, defuses Creeper explosion fuses, shields surrounding terrain from blast damage, and suppresses hostile spawns.
* **The Atlas Market**: Access an expansive 5-column categorized planar outpost offering dimensional relics, exotic trade goods, and rare blueprints.
* **Astral Infuser & Armor**: Channel Astral Emeralds into the Astral Infuser workstation to craft cosmic Astral Armor radiating amethyst particles with enhanced protection.

### 🪧 Real-Time In-World Signboards
* **In-World Signboard Editor**: Place wooden signs anywhere. Right-clicking opens an in-world speech bubble anchored above the signpost with smooth camera tracking.
* **Live Typing & Auto-Save**: Type in real-time with a blinking cursor (`|`), multiline support, and seamless auto-saving (press Enter, right-click outside, or walk away).
* **Hover Tooltips & Multiplayer Sync**: Inspect signs from afar with hover tooltips; all edits and placements sync instantaneously across multiplayer.

### 🎨 Profile Customization & Cosmetics
* **Discord-Style Profile Card**: Showcase your identity with collectible avatar frames, profile banners, crafter titles, Planar Tier badges, and Astral Gem counters.
* **Cosmetics Catalog**: Unlock cosmetic customizations that persist across singleplayer and multiplayer sessions.

### 🎵 Custom Audio, Jukeboxes & Music Discs
* **13 Vinyl Discs**: Collect rare music records throughout the world and play them in craftable Jukebox blocks with ambient particle effects.
* **Custom MP3 Player**: Import local MP3 files directly into your in-game Jukeboxes. Audio files persist locally via IndexedDB storage.
* **Floating Audio HUD**: Track music playback with interactive seek bars, volume controls, and real-time audio waveform visualizers.

### 🔥 Smelting & Container Storage
* **Dynamic Furnaces**: Smelt ores into refined ingots, transform sand into glass, and cook raw food into nutritious meals. Furnaces feature lit/unlit states and cast dynamic light while burning fuel.
* **Container Inventories**: Store items across Small (27-slot) and Large Double (54-slot) chests with deep serialization ensuring zero item loss across game exits and restarts.

### 🎮 Native Gamepad & Controller Support
* **Plug-and-Play Compatibility**: Full support for Xbox, PlayStation DualShock/DualSense, Nintendo Switch Pro, and standard Bluetooth/USB gamepads.
* **Dual Analog Controls**: Smooth platformer movement with the left stick and 360° crosshair aiming with the right stick.
* **Spatial UI Navigation**: Navigate hotbars, inventory slots, settings, and crafting grids using D-Pad and analog stick focus navigation.
* **Haptic Feedback**: Dual-motor vibration support for block breaking, taking damage, and tool feedback.

### ⚙️ World Modes, Graphics & Quality of Life
* **Multiple Difficulties**: Play on **Peaceful** (no hostile spawns or hunger), **Easy**, **Normal**, **Hard**, or **Hardcore** (permadeath).
* **Fabulous Graphics Settings**: Toggleable volumetric god rays, wind breeze foliage sway, desert heat shimmer, ambient fireflies, and ambient occlusion.
* **Dynamic FPS Cap**: Choose between 30, 60, 120, 144 FPS or Unlimited to match your monitor and optimize performance.
* **Recipe Pinning HUD**: Pin crafting requirements directly to your HUD to track materials in real time while exploring.
* **Dynamic Theme Engines**: Customize UI accent colors, focus rings, and glowing elements with dynamic color palettes.

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Rendering Engine**: Native HTML5 `<canvas>` API with custom pixel-art render passes.
* **Language**: Vanilla Modern JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, and CSS3.
* **Audio Engine**: Web Audio API & Web Storage (IndexedDB) for persistent local music tracks.
* **Input Engine**: Gamepad API for hardware controller support and rumble feedback.
* **Storage**: LocalStorage & IndexedDB for saving world states, inventory contents, difficulty settings, and custom audio files.
